Test your heating before winter

By James Gartshore · 28 September 2026 · Everyday tech

In short

  • Cold radiators: Radiators staying cold, or only warm at the bottom or top.
  • Strange noises: Banging, gurgling or whistling from the boiler or radiators.
  • Switching off: The heating cutting out by itself before it should.
  • Thermostat not working: Turning it up or down makes no difference.
  • Error codes: A code or flashing light on the boiler's display.
  • Smells or leaks: Any odd smell, drips or damp patches near pipes.

Hi everyone, with winter approaching, now is a good time to test your heating, especially if it hasn't been used much this year.

Try putting your heating on for at least an hour, with the thermostat set high enough for it to come on and warm your home. While it's running, keep an eye and an ear out for any problems, such as radiators staying cold or only heating partially, unusual noises from the boiler or radiators, the heating switching off unexpectedly, the thermostat not responding, error codes on the boiler, or strange smells or leaks.

If you do spot a problem, it's worth getting it looked at now rather than waiting until winter. It could be cheaper to sort it out early, and once the cold weather arrives, heating engineers can get very busy, with appointments and call-outs becoming harder to get.

A quick check now could save you a lot of hassle when you really need the heating.
