Scary pop-up on your phone? It's just an advert

By James Gartshore · 09 October 2026 · Scams and staying safe

In short

  • Wait for the X: An X usually appears in a top corner within about 30 seconds. Tap it.
  • Don't tap the ad: Ignore buttons like "Clean now" or "Fix", and don't install what it offers.
  • Check before you install: The Play Store shows "Contains ads" under an app's name.

Hi everyone, I wanted to share a tip about those scary pop-ups that sometimes appear on phones.

If you're using an app or playing a game and a message suddenly pops up saying your phone is infected, has been hacked or is damaged, don't panic. It's just an advert. Lots of free apps make their money by showing ads, and some advertisers use these fake warnings to frighten people into tapping them. Your phone is fine.

Whatever you do, don't tap anything inside the ad, like "Clean now", "Fix" or "Remove virus", and don't install anything it offers or call any number it shows. Just wait. An X usually appears in the top left or right corner, normally within about 30 seconds, and tapping it will close the ad. Sometimes the X is very small or faint, so look carefully.

If no X appears, you can close the app completely. On most Android phones, swipe up from the bottom of the screen and hold your finger there for a moment, or tap the button at the bottom that looks like three lines or a square. This shows all your open apps. Find the one with the ad and swipe it up off the screen to close it. On an iPhone, swipe up from the bottom and pause, then swipe the app up in the same way. The same pop-ups can also appear on websites, and in that case just close the tab or the browser.

If you'd rather not see ads at all, check an app's page in the Play Store before installing it. If it shows "Contains ads" under the app's name, you'll get adverts while using it. Some apps offer a small one-off payment to remove them.

If these pop-ups keep appearing when you're not using any app at all, that's a different problem, and it usually means a bad app has been installed. I'll be sharing how to find and remove it in another post, but in the meantime feel free to comment below and I'll do my best to help.

I hope this helps, and feel free to pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
