National Databank Free SIM

By James Gartshore · 28 October 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, did you know there's free mobile data for people who can't afford it? The National Databank, run by the charity Good Things Foundation, gives out free SIM cards with data, texts and calls through local libraries and community groups.

1. Check if you can get one. You need to be 18 or over, on a low income, and have no internet at home, no internet when you're out, or be unable to afford your phone contract or top-ups. 2. Ask at your local library or community centre for the National Databank. You can search "Good Things find a centre" online to find places near you. 3. They give you a free SIM card from O2, Vodafone or Three. There's no contract. 4. Pop it into your phone using the little SIM tray. Your phone needs to be unlocked, or locked to the same network.

How much data you get, and for how long, depends on the network and your area. Three SIM cards are data only, with no calls or texts.
