No signal? The Airplane mode trick

In this lesson

  • How to turn Airplane mode on and off
  • When it helps
  • What to try next

If your phone suddenly says No service, or the internet stops working, turning Airplane mode on and off makes it reconnect. It's quicker than a restart.

Good to know

  • Wait 10 seconds before turning it off again.
  • Still nothing? Restart the phone, then check your network's website for problems.
  • Abroad or on a plane : Airplane mode stops roaming charges. You can turn Wi-Fi back on.
  • It's in quick settings : swipe down from the top (top-right on iPhone).

Try it yourself: get your signal back

The practice phone has no service. Use Airplane mode to fix it.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Swipe down from the top-right corner. Tap the plane, wait 10 seconds, tap it again.

Android: Swipe down from the top. Tap Airplane mode, wait 10 seconds, tap it again.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Airplane mode on, wait, then off.
  • It makes the phone reconnect.
  • Still no signal? Restart.
  • Handy abroad to avoid roaming.
