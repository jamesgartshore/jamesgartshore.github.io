Protecting your phone from thieves

In this lesson

  • How to turn on theft protection
  • How to hide codes from the lock screen
  • How to find your IMEI number

Phone thieves are after your banking apps as much as the phone. A few settings make a stolen phone much less use to them, and knowing your IMEI number helps your network block it.

Good to know

  • Stolen Device Protection (iPhone) stops someone who's seen your passcode changing your Apple Account.
  • Theft Detection Lock (Android) locks the screen if someone snatches it and runs.
  • Hide message previews so nobody can read your security codes on the lock screen.
  • Dial *#06# to see your IMEI number, and write it down somewhere safe.

Try it yourself: lock it down

Turn on theft protection, hide previews and find your IMEI.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Settings > Face ID & Passcode > Stolen Device Protection: turn on. Settings > Notifications > Show Previews > When Unlocked. Phone app: dial *#06#.

Android (Google Pixel): Settings > Google > All services > Theft protection: turn on Theft Detection Lock. Settings > Notifications > Notifications on lock screen > Hide sensitive content. Phone app: dial *#06#.

Samsung: Theft protection is in Settings > Google or Security and privacy. Lock screen notifications: Settings > Notifications > Lock screen notifications.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Turn on Stolen Device Protection or Theft Detection Lock.
  • Hide message previews on the lock screen.
  • Dial *#06# for your IMEI and write it down.
  • If stolen: ring your bank first.
