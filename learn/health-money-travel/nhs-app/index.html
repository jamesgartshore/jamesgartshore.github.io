The NHS App

In this lesson

  • What the NHS App can do
  • How to order a repeat prescription
  • How to spot NHS scam texts

The NHS App is free and lets you order repeat prescriptions, see GP appointments and test results, and get messages from your surgery, without phoning.

Good to know

  • It's free from the App Store or Play Store, made by NHS England.
  • NHS login checks your identity once, with photo ID.
  • Repeat prescriptions usually take 2 to 3 working days.
  • Scam texts pretending to be the NHS ask for bank details. The NHS never does.

Try it yourself: order a repeat prescription

Order a repeat prescription, then check your appointments, on a pretend NHS App.

The steps, written out

iPhone and Android: NHS App > Prescriptions > Order a repeat prescription > choose medicines > Continue > Confirm and order. Appointments: the Appointments section.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • The NHS App is free, made by NHS England.
  • Order repeat prescriptions without phoning.
  • Check appointments and messages from your GP.
  • The NHS never asks for bank details by text.
