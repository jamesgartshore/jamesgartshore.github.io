Finding your way around

In this lesson

  • Where to find your apps
  • What the three window buttons do
  • How to bring back a hidden window

Every computer has a desktop, a place to start apps from, and windows that open on top. Once you know the three window buttons, you can find your way around almost anything.

Good to know

  • Windows : apps are in Start (bottom of the screen). Window buttons are top right: — tuck away, ▢ bigger, ✕ close.
  • Mac : apps are in the Dock (bottom) and Apps. Window buttons are top left: red close, yellow tuck away, green bigger.
  • Linux Mint : apps are in the Menu (bottom left). Window buttons are top right, like Windows.
  • Lost a window? Click its icon on the taskbar, Dock or panel to bring it back.

Try it yourself: open, hide and close a window

Open the web browser, tuck it away, bring it back and close it.

The steps, written out

Windows: Start > click an app. Use — ▢ ✕ at the top right of the window.

Mac: Click an app in the Dock. Use the red, yellow and green buttons at the top left. ⌘ Cmd + Q quits an app.

Linux Mint: Menu (bottom left) > click an app. Use the buttons at the top right of the window.

Menu names can differ slightly between versions and updates.

Remember

  • Start, the Dock or the Menu holds your apps.
  • Minimise tucks a window away.
  • Click the app's icon to bring it back.
  • Close with ✕ (or red on a Mac).
