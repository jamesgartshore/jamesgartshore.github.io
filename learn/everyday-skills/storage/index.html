Storage full? Free up space

In this lesson

  • How to see what's filling your phone
  • How to clear big WhatsApp videos
  • How to remove unused apps safely

"Storage almost full" means your phone can't take photos or install updates. The usual culprits are videos people send you on WhatsApp, and apps you never use. You can clear them without losing anything you care about.

Good to know

  • Settings shows what's using space : Settings > General > iPhone Storage, or Settings > Storage on Android.
  • WhatsApp videos are often the biggest thing. WhatsApp's Manage storage finds them.
  • Recently Deleted in Photos keeps deleted photos for 30 days. Empty it to get the space back.
  • Don't pay for "cleaner" apps . Your phone has everything you need built in.

Try it yourself: free up space

Find what's using space, then clear big WhatsApp videos.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Settings > General > iPhone Storage. Turn on Offload Unused Apps. Then WhatsApp > Settings > Storage and data > Manage storage > Larger than 5 MB.

Android (Google Pixel): Settings > Storage > Free up space. Then WhatsApp > ⋮ > Settings > Storage and data > Manage storage.

Samsung: Settings > Battery and device care > Storage. Or open the My Files app > Analyse storage.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Check Storage in Settings to see what's using space.
  • WhatsApp videos are often the biggest culprit.
  • Empty Recently Deleted in Photos.
  • Don't pay for cleaner apps.
