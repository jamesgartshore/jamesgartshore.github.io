Alarms and tablet reminders

In this lesson

  • How to set an alarm
  • How to make it repeat every day
  • How to label it so you know what it's for

Your phone can remind you to take your tablets, at the same time every day. An alarm with a label tells you exactly what it's for.

Good to know

  • Repeat makes the alarm go off every day (or just weekdays).
  • Label shows on screen when it rings, like "Tablets" or "Bins out".
  • Stop or Snooze : Stop turns it off until next time; Snooze rings again in a few minutes.
  • iPhone Health app has a Medications section that reminds you and keeps a log of what you've taken.

Try it yourself: set a tablets alarm

Set an alarm for 8 o'clock every day, labelled Tablets.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Clock > Alarms > +. Set the time, tap Repeat > choose the days, type a Label, tap Save.

Android (Google Pixel): Clock > Alarm > +. Set the time, tick the days, tap Add label, then OK.

Samsung: Clock > Alarm > +. Set the time, tap the days, type an Alarm name, tap Save.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Alarms are in the Clock app.
  • Repeat makes it go off every day.
  • A label says what it's for.
  • Stop turns it off until next time.
