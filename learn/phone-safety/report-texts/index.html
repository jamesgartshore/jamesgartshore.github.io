Reporting scam texts to 7726

In this lesson

  • How to forward a scam text to 7726
  • Why reporting helps everyone
  • Where to report scam emails

7726 is a free number that every UK mobile network uses to collect scam texts. Forwarding them helps block the scammers for everyone. (7726 spells SPAM on a keypad.)

Good to know

  • It's free on every UK network.
  • Don't tap the link in the scam text.
  • After reporting , delete it and block the number.
  • Scam emails go to report@phishing.gov.uk instead.

Try it yourself: report a scam text

Forward the fake Royal Mail text to 7726, then tell it which number it came from.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Press and hold the message > More > forward arrow > type 7726 > send. Then reply with the scammer's number.

Android (Google Pixel): Press and hold the message > ⋮ > Forward > type 7726 > send. Then reply with the number.

Samsung: Press and hold the message > Forward > 7726.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Forward scam texts to 7726, for free.
  • Then send the number it came from.
  • Never tap links in scam texts.
  • Scam emails go to report@phishing.gov.uk.
