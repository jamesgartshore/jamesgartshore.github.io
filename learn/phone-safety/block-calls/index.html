Blocking nuisance calls

In this lesson

  • How to block a number
  • How to silence or filter unknown callers
  • Why you should still check voicemail

If the same number keeps ringing, you can block it in a few taps. You can also stop calls from unknown numbers ringing your phone at all.

Good to know

  • Blocked numbers can't call or text you.
  • Silencing unknown callers sends them to voicemail; they still show in your recent calls.
  • Genuine callers like hospitals usually leave a voicemail, so check it.
  • BT landlines : dial 1572 to block the last caller.

Try it yourself: block a nuisance number

Block the number that keeps calling, then cut down unknown calls.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Phone > Recents > ⓘ next to the number > Block this Caller. Silence unknown callers: Settings > Apps > Phone > Silence Unknown Callers.

Android (Google Pixel): Phone > Recents > tap the number > Block/report spam. Filter spam: Phone > ⋮ > Settings > Caller ID & spam.

Samsung: Phone > Recents > tap the number > ⓘ > Block. Settings > Block numbers.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Block a number from your recent calls.
  • Silence or filter unknown callers to cut down scam calls.
  • Check your voicemail now and then.
  • Report spam to help warn others.
