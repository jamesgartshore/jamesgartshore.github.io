Keeping your phone safe

Block nuisance calls, report scam texts, keep your phone updated, set up your Medical ID and find a lost phone.

Who it's for: Everyone with a smartphone, especially if you get nuisance calls or scam texts.

Start the course

Lessons

  1. 1 Blocking nuisance callsBlock a number that keeps calling, and silence or filter unknown callers. Status: 5 min
  2. 2 Reporting scam texts to 7726Forward scam texts to 7726, for free, so networks can block them. Status: 5 min
  3. 3 Software updatesKeep your phone updated to fix security problems, and turn on automatic updates. Status: 5 min
  4. 4 Medical ID and emergency contactsStore allergies, conditions and an emergency contact that paramedics can see. Status: 6 min
  5. 5 Finding a lost phoneSwitch on Find My or Find Hub, then make a lost phone ring or lock it. Status: 5 min
