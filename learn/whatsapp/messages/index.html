Messages, photos and voice notes

In this lesson

  • How to start a chat and send a message
  • How to send a photo and a voice note
  • What the grey and blue ticks mean

Now WhatsApp is set up, it's time to use it. Messages are free over Wi-Fi or mobile data, however long they are, and you can send photos and voice notes too.

What the ticks mean

  • One grey tick ✓: your message has been sent.
  • Two grey ticks ✓✓: it has arrived on the other person's phone.
  • Two blue ticks ✓✓: they've read it.

Handy things to know

  • Voice notes are great if typing is fiddly: press and hold the microphone while you talk, and let go to send.
  • Sent something by mistake? Press and hold the message, then choose Delete and Delete for everyone. It works for a while after sending.
  • To reply to one particular message, swipe it to the right, then type your reply.
  • Photos you're sent are saved on your phone, so think before forwarding them on.

Try it yourself: message, photo and voice note

On the practice phone, start a chat with Sarah, then send her a message, a photo and a voice note. Type anything you like: it isn't sent anywhere.

The steps, written out

  1. New chat: on iPhone, tap the pencil at the top right; on Android, tap the green button at the bottom right. Then tap the person's name.
  2. Message: tap the box at the bottom, type, and tap the send arrow.
  3. Photo: tap the + (iPhone) or paperclip (Android), choose Photos or Gallery, tap a photo, add a caption if you like, and tap send.
  4. Voice note: press and hold the microphone, talk, then let go to send.

Remember

  • Tap the new chat button, then the person's name, to start a chat.
  • One grey tick means sent, two grey ticks delivered, two blue ticks read.
  • Press and hold the microphone to record a voice note, and let go to send.
  • Press and hold a message to delete it, for you or for everyone.
