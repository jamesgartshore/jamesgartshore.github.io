Video calls and group chats

In this lesson

  • How to make a free video or voice call
  • How to mute yourself and flip the camera
  • How to mute or leave a busy group

WhatsApp calls are free, and video calls let you see the grandchildren from anywhere in the world. Groups let the whole family, a club or your neighbours chat together.

Calls

  • Video or voice: open the person's chat and tap the video camera for a video call, or the phone for a voice call.
  • Use Wi-Fi when you can. Video calls use a lot of data, but on Wi-Fi they don't use your mobile allowance.
  • During a call you can mute yourself, turn your camera off, or flip it to show what's in front of you.
  • Hold the phone at eye level, with the light in front of you rather than behind, so people can see your face.

Groups

  • Busy group? Mute it so your phone doesn't buzz for every message. You'll still see the messages when you open WhatsApp.
  • Everyone in a group sees what you write, and your phone number, even people you don't know.
  • You can leave any group: tap its name at the top, then Exit group.

Try it yourself: a video call and a busy group

Make a pretend video call to Sarah, then mute the noisy Family group.

The steps, written out

  1. Video call: open the person's chat and tap the video camera at the top. Tap the red button to end the call.
  2. Mute yourself: tap the microphone during the call. Tap it again to unmute.
  3. Mute a group: open the group, tap its name at the top, tap Mute notifications, and choose how long for.

Remember

  • Open a chat and tap the video camera for a video call, or the phone for a voice call.
  • Use Wi-Fi for video calls when you can, to save your mobile data.
  • Mute a busy group so your phone doesn't buzz for every message.
  • Everyone in a group can see your phone number.
