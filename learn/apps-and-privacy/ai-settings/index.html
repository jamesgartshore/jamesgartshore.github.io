Turning off AI features

In this lesson

  • How to turn off Apple Intelligence or Gemini
  • How to stop AI keeping what you type
  • What not to share with any AI chat

New phones come with AI features built in: Apple Intelligence on newer iPhones, Gemini on Android, and Galaxy AI on Samsung. Some people find them useful; others would rather not have them. You can turn them off, and turn them back on any time.

Good to know

  • Apple Intelligence is only on newer iPhones (iPhone 15 Pro and later). If you don't see it in Settings, your phone doesn't have it.
  • Holding the power button may open Gemini on Android. Setting the assistant to None stops that.
  • AI chats in WhatsApp and Facebook can't always be switched off, but you don't have to use them.
  • Keep personal details out of any AI chat: bank, health, passwords or addresses.

Try it yourself: switch AI off

Turn off the AI features on the practice phone.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri > turn off Apple Intelligence. Tap ChatGPT > turn off Use ChatGPT.

Android (Google Pixel): Settings > Apps > Default apps > Digital assistant app > choose None. In the Gemini app: profile picture > Gemini Apps Activity (or Keep Activity) > Turn off and delete activity.

Samsung: Settings > Galaxy AI: each feature has its own switch. Also turn on "Process data only on device".

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • AI features can be turned off, and back on.
  • iPhone: Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri.
  • Android: set the assistant to None, and turn off Gemini activity.
  • Never share personal details with an AI chat.
