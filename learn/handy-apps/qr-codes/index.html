Scanning QR codes

In this lesson

  • How to scan a QR code with your camera
  • How to check where it goes
  • How to spot fake QR codes

QR codes are square patterns that open a web page when you point your camera at them. They're on posters, menus and letters, and your camera reads them, with no extra app.

Good to know

  • Point and wait : a link appears on the screen.
  • Check the link before you tap it.
  • Car park stickers : scammers stick fake codes over real ones.
  • Android : if the camera doesn't, try Google Lens.

Try it yourself: scan a QR code

Point the practice camera at the poster and open the link.

The steps, written out

iPhone: Open the Camera, point it at the code, and tap the yellow link.

Android (Google Pixel): Open the Camera (or Google Lens), point it at the code, and tap the link.

Samsung: Camera app, or swipe down and tap Scan QR code.

Menu names can differ slightly between phone models and updates.

Remember

  • Your camera reads QR codes.
  • Check the link before tapping.
  • Be wary of QR stickers on car park machines.
  • Use the official app or sign to pay.
