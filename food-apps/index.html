Save money on food with your phone

By James Gartshore · 16 October 2026 · Money

In short

  • Too Good To Go: Buy "surprise bags" of unsold food from local shops, bakeries and cafes at a big discount. (Free app)
  • Olio: Neighbours give away spare food and household items for free. (Free app)
  • Supermarket apps: Unlock member prices and personal offers at the big supermarkets. (Tesco Clubcard, Nectar, My Morrisons and more)
  • Supercook: Tick off what's in your fridge and it finds recipes you can make with it. (Free at supercook.com)

Hi everyone, today is World Food Day, so I wanted to share some free apps that can help you save money on food and stop good food from going to waste.

Too Good To Go lets you buy "surprise bags" of food that shops, bakeries, cafes and supermarkets haven't sold by the end of the day, usually for around a third of the normal price. You don't know exactly what you'll get, which is part of the fun, and you collect it at a set time. It's a great way to try somewhere new for less.

Olio is a free app where neighbours give away food they don't need, like spare vegetables from the garden, things they've bought too much of, or items they won't use before the date runs out. Lots of people also use it to give away household items for free, so it's worth a look.

If you shop at the big supermarkets, it's worth getting their app. Many prices are now only available if you have the loyalty card or app, like Tesco Clubcard Prices or Nectar Prices at Sainsbury's, and you'll often get personal offers and money off vouchers too.

And before you buy anything new, Supercook can help you make the most of what you've already got. It's a free website at supercook.com where you tick off the ingredients in your fridge and cupboards, and it finds recipes you can make with them. It's a great way to use up odds and ends before they go out of date.

If you'd like help setting any of these apps up, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.

I hope this helps, and feel free to pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
