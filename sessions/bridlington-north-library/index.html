Free drop-in tech help at Bridlington North Library

Let's Tech Together runs free drop-in tech help sessions at Bridlington North Library, and I volunteer at them. Bring along your phone, laptop, iPad or tablet, whether something isn't working, you'd like to learn something new, or you just have a question. You don't need to bring a device to ask something. It's free, there's no need to book, and there are no stupid questions.

Please note: this website and everything on it are my own personal project. They are not run by, connected to or endorsed by Let's Tech Together, and these are not official Let's Tech Together pages. I'm simply showing where you can find me when I'm volunteering at their free drop-in sessions. For official information, please contact Let's Tech Together directly. Dates can change, so it's worth checking with them before you travel.
