Bright Sky: free, private help with domestic abuse

By James Gartshore · 25 November 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, today is the UN’s International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. I wanted to share a free app that could quietly help someone.

1. Bright Sky is a free app from the charity Hestia. Search for Bright Sky in your app store. 2. It has questions to help someone think about whether a relationship is safe, and the signs of abuse. 3. It finds specialist support nearby by postcode or location, and you can ring them from the app. 4. It’s also for anyone worried about a friend or family member, with advice on how to help.

In an emergency, call 999. If you can’t speak on a mobile, stay on the line and press 55 when asked. The National Domestic Abuse Helpline is free and open 24 hours on 0808 2000 247.
