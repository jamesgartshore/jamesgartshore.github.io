Send a Photo on WhatsApp

By James Gartshore · 09 November 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, here's how to send a photo to family on WhatsApp, whether it's the grandchildren, the garden or a letter you need help with.

1. Open WhatsApp and tap the chat with the person. 2. Tap the paperclip next to where you type your message. 3. Tap Gallery. 4. Tap the photo you want, then tap the green arrow to send it. You can type a caption first if you like.

On an iPhone, tap the + next to the typing box, then Photos. For a normal text message, tap the + or the picture icon next to where you type.
