Move Files to a New Laptop

By James Gartshore · 08 November 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, got a new laptop? Here's how to bring your photos and documents across from your old one. All you need is a USB memory stick.

1. Plug a USB memory stick into your old laptop. Make sure it's big enough for your photos: 64 GB or more is a good size. 2. Open File Explorer (the yellow folder at the bottom of the screen) and copy your Documents, Pictures, Desktop and Downloads folders onto the stick. To copy, click a folder, press Ctrl and C, then open the USB stick and press Ctrl and V. 3. Plug the stick into your new laptop and copy the folders across the same way. 4. Sign in to your web browser, like Chrome or Edge, with the same account you used before. Your bookmarks and saved passwords come across too.

If you used OneDrive on your old laptop, just sign in with the same Microsoft account on the new one and your files will appear.

Once everything's safely on the new laptop, remember to wipe the old one before you pass it on.
