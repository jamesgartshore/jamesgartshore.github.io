Delete Emails on Your Phone

By James Gartshore · 31 October 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, is your email inbox full of old messages, offers and newsletters? Here's how to clear them out on your phone, using the Gmail app.

1. To delete one email, open it, then tap the bin at the top of the screen. 2. To delete lots at once, tap the little picture (or circle) next to each email you want to get rid of. A tick appears. 3. Tap the bin at the top. They all go at once. 4. Deleted emails go into the Bin, and are removed for good after 30 days. To free up space straight away, tap the 3 lines at the top left, tap Bin, then Empty Bin now.

If you see the word "Archive" instead, the email isn't deleted, just tidied away out of your inbox.

On an iPhone using the Mail app, swipe left on an email and tap Bin. To delete lots at once, tap Edit, tick the emails, then tap Bin.
