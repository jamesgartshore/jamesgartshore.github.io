Dyspraxia: when handwriting is hard

By James Gartshore · 14 October 2026 · Health and wellbeing

In short

  • Voice typing in Google Docs: Speak and your words appear on the page, ready to tidy up. (Free: Tools > Voice typing)
  • Dictate in Word: The same idea in Microsoft Word, with punctuation by voice. (Home tab > Dictate)
  • Swipe typing: Slide a finger across the letters instead of tapping each one. (Built into Gboard, SwiftKey and iPhone)
  • Google Lens: Take a photo of the board or a worksheet and copy the text. (Free in the Google app or camera)

Hi everyone, it's day three of Dyspraxia Awareness Week, and today's tips are for anyone who finds handwriting hard.

Many people with dyspraxia find handwriting slow, tiring and sometimes painful, and it can make it hard to get their ideas down on paper. There are some free tools that can take the strain.

Google Docs has a free voice typing feature. Open a document in the Chrome browser, go to the Tools menu and choose Voice typing, then click the microphone and start talking. Your words appear on the page, and you can go back and tidy them up afterwards. Microsoft Word has the same idea, called Dictate, which you'll find on the Home tab. You can even say things like "full stop" and "new line" as you go.

On a phone, swipe typing can make typing much easier than tapping each letter. Instead of pressing every key, you slide your finger from letter to letter and lift it at the end of each word. It's built into Gboard and SwiftKey on Android, and into the standard iPhone keyboard. It feels a bit strange at first, but lots of people find it quicker and less fiddly once they get used to it.

Google Lens is another handy one. If you need to copy something from a whiteboard, a worksheet or a letter, just take a photo with Google Lens and it can pick out the text so you can copy it into a document or message, rather than writing it all out by hand. You'll find it in the Google app, and on many Android phones it's built into the camera.

If you'd like help setting any of these up, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.
