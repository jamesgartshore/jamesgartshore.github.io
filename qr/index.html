What is a QR code?

By James Gartshore · 06 October 2026 · Getting online and staying connected

In short

  • What does it do?: Opens a webpage for you. No typing or searching.
  • Where are they?: NHS letters, menus, bus stops and leaflets.
  • Is it free?: Yes. Nothing to pay and nothing extra to download.

Hi everyone, I just wanted to share a quick tip about QR codes.

You don't need to download an app to scan them. Almost every smartphone already has a QR scanner built into the camera. Just open your camera, point it at the code, and a link will pop up for you to tap.

It's best to avoid the QR scanner apps in the app store. A lot of them are full of adverts or collect your personal information, and some have even been found to contain viruses that can harm your phone. Your camera does the same job for free and is much safer.

I hope this helps, and feel free to pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
