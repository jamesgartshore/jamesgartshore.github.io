Ads popping up out of nowhere?

By James Gartshore · 10 October 2026

Hi everyone, following on from my post about scary pop-up adverts, here's what to do if ads keep appearing on your Android phone even when you're not using any app.

If you're seeing adverts on your home screen, over the top of other apps, or as soon as you unlock your phone, a bad app has probably been installed. These usually sneak on through free apps like torch apps, phone cleaners or games, or by tapping an advert that installs something without you realising. They often give themselves dull names like "Keyboard Settings" or "System Service", and many hide their icon so you don't notice them on your home screen.

The easiest way to track one down is through your battery settings. Because these apps run in the background all the time, they tend to use more battery than they should. Open Settings, tap Battery, then Battery usage. On some Samsung phones you may need to tap the graph or "View details" to see the list of apps. Look for an app near the top that you don't recognise or never use. Don't worry about things like Screen, WhatsApp or YouTube, as it's normal for those to use battery. Make a note of the name of anything that looks out of place.

Next, go back to Settings, tap Apps, and find the app in the list. Tap it, then tap Uninstall. If the Uninstall button is greyed out, tap Disable or Force stop instead. Some bad apps give themselves extra permissions to stop you removing them. If that happens, search your settings for "Device admin apps", switch the app off there, then go back and uninstall it.

Once it's gone, it's a good idea to run a quick check by opening the Play Store, tapping your profile picture, then Play Protect, and tapping Scan.

If you're not sure whether an app is safe to remove, or you can't find the one causing the problem, just comment below and I'll do my best to help.

I hope this helps, and feel free to pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
