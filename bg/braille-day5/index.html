Control your phone with your voice

От James Gartshore · 18 октомври 2026 · Здраве и благополучие

Кратко за

  • Voice Access: Control your whole Android phone by speaking. (Android: Settings > Accessibility)
  • Voice Control: Open apps, tap buttons and type, all by voice. (iPhone: Settings > Accessibility)
  • Siri and Google Assistant: "Call Sarah", "read my messages", "what's the time?" (Built into every phone)
  • Smart speakers: Ask for the news, the weather, the radio or a timer. (Amazon Echo or Google Nest)

Hi everyone, it's day five of National Braille Week, and today I wanted to share how you can control your phone using just your voice.

Android phones have a free feature called Voice Access, which lets you control the whole phone by speaking. You can say things like "open WhatsApp", "scroll down" or "tap send". You'll find it in Settings under Accessibility.

iPhones have something similar called Voice Control. Once it's on, you can open apps, tap buttons and type messages, all by voice. You'll find it in Settings, then Accessibility, then Voice Control.

For everyday things, Siri on iPhone and Google Assistant on Android can do a lot without you needing to see the screen at all. Try saying "call Sarah", "read my messages", "what's the time?" or "remind me to take my tablets at 6pm".

And smart speakers like the Amazon Echo or Google Nest are brilliant if you have sight loss. You can ask them for the news, the weather, your favourite radio station, a timer for cooking, or even to call someone.
