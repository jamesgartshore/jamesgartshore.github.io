Dyspraxia: help for adults and students

By James Gartshore · 18 October 2026 · Health and wellbeing

Кратко за

  • Dyspraxia Foundation: Advice, guides and support groups for children and adults. (dyspraxiafoundation.org.uk)
  • Access to Work: A government grant that can pay for equipment and software at work. (Search "Access to Work" on gov.uk)
  • Disabled Students' Allowance: Can pay for laptops and software for university students. (Search "DSA" on gov.uk)
  • Voice assistants: Set timers, reminders and shopping lists without lifting a pen. (Siri, Google Assistant or Alexa)

Hi everyone, it's the last day of Dyspraxia Awareness Week, and I wanted to finish by sharing some support for adults and students, because dyspraxia doesn't stop at school.

The Dyspraxia Foundation is a UK charity with lots of helpful advice and guides for children, parents and adults, as well as support groups where you can meet others who understand. You'll find them at dyspraxiafoundation.org.uk.

If you have dyspraxia and you're working, or about to start a job, Access to Work is a government grant that can help pay for things that make work easier. This can include equipment, software like voice typing or mind mapping programs, or support with travel. It doesn't affect any other benefits, and your employer doesn't pay for it. You can find out more and apply by searching "Access to Work" on gov.uk.

For students going to university or college, Disabled Students' Allowance, often called DSA, can help pay for things like a laptop, specialist software and other support while you study. It doesn't need to be paid back. Search "Disabled Students' Allowance" on gov.uk to find out more.

And voice assistants like Siri, Google Assistant and Alexa can help with everyday life too. You can set timers when cooking, add reminders, and build up a shopping list just by speaking, without having to write anything down.

Thank you to everyone who's followed my posts this week. I hope some of these tips have been useful. If you have any questions, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.

Feel free to pass this on to anyone who might find it useful.
