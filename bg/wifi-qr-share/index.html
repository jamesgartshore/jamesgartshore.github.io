Share Wi-Fi with a QR Code

By James Gartshore · 22 October 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, when family or friends visit and ask for your Wi-Fi password, there's no need to read out a long jumble of letters and numbers. Most Android phones can show a square QR code instead, and your visitor just points their camera at it.

On a Samsung phone: 1. Open Settings, then tap Connections. 2. Tap Wi-Fi, then tap the little cog next to the network you're connected to. 3. Tap QR code at the bottom of the screen (on some phones it's called Share). 4. Your phone may ask you to show it's you, with your PIN, pattern or fingerprint. 5. A QR code appears. Your visitor opens their phone's camera, points it at the code, and taps the message that pops up to join.

On other Android phones, such as Google Pixel, go to Settings, then Network & internet, then Internet, and tap the cog next to your Wi-Fi. Then tap Share.

It works for visitors with an iPhone too, as the iPhone camera can read these codes.
