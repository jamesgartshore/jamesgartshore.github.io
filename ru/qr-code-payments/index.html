QR Code Payment Safety

By James Gartshore · 25 October 2026 · Getting online and staying connected

Hi everyone, a quick warning about QR codes, those square barcodes you scan with your phone camera. Scammers stick fake ones over real ones, especially on car park machines, so you end up typing your card details into a fake website.

1. Before you scan, look and feel for a sticker stuck on top of the code. That's a big warning sign. 2. When you point your camera at a code, your phone shows the web address before it opens. Read it. If it doesn't look like the council or company you're paying, don't open it. 3. The safest way to pay for parking is at the machine with your card or cash, or with the official parking app downloaded from your app store. 4. If a letter, email or text you weren't expecting has a QR code asking you to pay a fine or bill, don't scan it. Go to the official website yourself instead.

Many councils say they don't use QR codes for parking at all.

If you think you've paid a scammer, ring your bank straight away (or dial 159), and report it to Report Fraud.
