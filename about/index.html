Hi, I'm James

I live in Bridlington and volunteer with a local tech support group, which helps people with their phones, computers and getting online. In 2026 I was shortlisted in the Time2Volunteer Awards for my volunteering.

This site is something separate that I do in my own time. I share free, plain-English tech tips, from spotting scam texts to making your phone easier to see, in the hope they reach people who might not otherwise know where to turn. You'll find the same tips on my Nextdoor profile.

I also design posters for free for local people, groups and events, sized properly for Facebook, Nextdoor or printing. You can see some examples on the design examples page.

Need hands-on help?

The tips here are a starting point. If you'd like someone to sit down with you and help in person, local tech support groups, drop-in sessions and your local library are the best places to go, and they're often free.

Who the tips are for

I write the tips with people in Bridlington, Driffield and across the East Riding of Yorkshire in mind, but they work wherever you are, so feel free to share them with friends and family further afield.
