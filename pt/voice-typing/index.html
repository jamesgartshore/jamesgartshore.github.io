Talk Instead of Typing

By James Gartshore · 12 November 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, if the keyboard on your phone is fiddly, you can talk to it instead and it writes your message for you.

1. Tap where you would type your message. 2. Tap the microphone on the keyboard. 3. Speak clearly, like you would on the phone. 4. Check the words, then tap send.

Tip: say "full stop", "comma" or "question mark" to add the punctuation.

On an iPhone, the microphone is at the bottom right of the keyboard.
