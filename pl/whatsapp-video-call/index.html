WhatsApp Video Call

By James Gartshore · 08 November 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, if you've got WhatsApp, you can video call your family for free over Wi-Fi and see each other's faces. Here's how.

1. Open WhatsApp. 2. Tap the chat with the person you want to call. 3. Tap the video camera icon at the top of the screen. 4. When they answer, you'll see each other. Tap the red button to hang up.

If you'd rather just talk, tap the phone icon next to the camera for a voice call instead. It works the same way on iPhone and Android.
