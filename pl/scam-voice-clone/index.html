Voice Clone Scam

By James Gartshore · 19 October 2026 · Scams and staying safe

Hi everyone, I wanted to share a warning about a scam that's becoming more common: phone calls using a copy of a family member's voice.

Scammers can now use AI to copy someone's voice from just a few seconds of audio, like a video posted on Facebook. They then ring a relative pretending to be that person, often in a panic: they've been in an accident, lost their phone, or need money urgently for a bill or a fine. Because it sounds just like someone you love, it's very easy to believe. One UK bank found that more than a quarter of people think they may already have been targeted.

The simplest protection is a family safe phrase. Agree a word or phrase with your close family, face to face, that only you know. If you ever get a worrying call asking for money, ask for the safe phrase. A scammer won't know it. Don't write it in a text or post it anywhere online.

If you're unsure, hang up and ring the person back on the number you already have for them, not a number the caller gives you. A real family member won't mind you checking. If the call is about your bank or your money, you can hang up and dial 159, which connects you safely to your own bank. It works with almost all the big UK banks.

Watch out for pressure. Being told it's urgent, that you mustn't tell anyone, or that you have to pay by bank transfer, gift cards or cash are all big warning signs. If you think you've been caught out, contact your bank straight away and report it at reportfraud.police.uk.

If you've had a call like this, or you'd like help setting up a safe phrase with your family, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.
