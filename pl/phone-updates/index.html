Phone Still Safe

By James Gartshore · 22 October 2026 · Scams and staying safe
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, I wanted to share a quick check you can do to see if your phone is still safe to use for things like banking and email.

Phones get free security updates from the company that made them, which fix weaknesses that scammers and hackers could use to get in. But they only get them for a certain number of years. After that, the phone still works, but it slowly becomes less safe, especially for banking, shopping and email.

First, make sure any waiting updates are installed. On an iPhone, go to Settings, General, then Software Update. On Android, it's usually in Settings, then System, then Software update. It's best to do it overnight while your phone is charging and on Wi-Fi.

On Android, you can also see how up to date your phone is. Go to Settings, then About phone, and look for "Security update" or "Android security patch level". It shows a date. If it's more than about six months ago and there's no update waiting, your phone may have stopped getting them. You can also search online for your phone's model followed by "security updates end date" to find out how long it'll be supported.

And if you or someone you know still uses a very old basic mobile phone, the 2G network those phones rely on is being switched off between 2029 and 2033. If your phone shows 4G or 5G at the top of the screen when you're not on Wi-Fi, you don't need to worry.

If you're not sure about your phone, send me a message with the make and model and I'll do my best to help you check.
