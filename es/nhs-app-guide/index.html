What the NHS App Can Do

By James Gartshore · 27 October 2026 · Health and wellbeing

Hi everyone, tired of ringing the doctors at 8am? The free NHS App puts lots of your GP services on your phone.

With the NHS App you can: 1. Order your repeat prescriptions, and choose which pharmacy to collect from. 2. Book and check GP appointments (if your surgery offers this). 3. See your test results and your GP health record. 4. Read messages and letters from your GP surgery and some hospitals.

It's free from the App Store or Google Play. When you first set it up, you'll create an NHS login, which needs photo ID such as a passport or driving licence. What you can do in the app depends on your GP surgery.

You can use it on a computer too: just search for "NHS App online".
