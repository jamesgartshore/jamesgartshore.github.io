Dyspraxia: tools that make reading easier

By James Gartshore · 15 October 2026 · Health and wellbeing

In short

  • Immersive Reader: Bigger spacing, line focus and read aloud to help you keep your place. (Free in Microsoft Edge, Word and OneNote)
  • BorrowBox and Libby: Free audiobooks and ebooks from your local library on your phone. (Free with a library card)
  • Reader view: Strips adverts and clutter from websites, leaving just the text. (Built into Safari, Edge and Firefox)
  • Dark mode: Light text on a dark background can be easier on tired eyes. (Settings > Display)

Hi everyone, it's day four of Dyspraxia Awareness Week, and today I wanted to share some free tools that make reading easier.

People with dyspraxia often find it hard to keep their place on a page, or find that their eyes get tired quickly when reading. A few simple tools can make a big difference.

Immersive Reader is a free tool built into Microsoft Edge, Word and OneNote. It can make text bigger and spread it out, highlight just one or a few lines at a time so you don't lose your place, and read the text aloud while highlighting each word. In Edge, look for the little book icon in the address bar on most web pages.

If reading a whole book feels like hard work, audiobooks are a brilliant alternative, and you can get them free from your local library. Apps like BorrowBox and Libby let you borrow audiobooks and ebooks on your phone or tablet using your library card. Which app you need depends on your library, so it's worth asking or checking their website.

Most web browsers also have a reader view, which strips away the adverts, pop-ups and clutter from a web page and leaves just the text. It's built into Safari, Edge and Firefox, and makes long articles much easier to read.

And if bright screens make your eyes tired, try switching on dark mode, which shows light text on a dark background. You'll find it in your phone or computer's Settings under Display.

If you'd like help setting any of these up, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.

I hope this helps, and feel free to pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
