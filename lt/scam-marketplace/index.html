Marketplace Scams

By James Gartshore · 21 November 2026 · Scams and staying safe
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, with Black Friday and Christmas shopping coming up, I wanted to share some tips for buying and selling safely on Facebook Marketplace and similar sites.

Purchase scams, where you pay for something that never arrives or doesn't exist, are now the most common scam in the UK. They made up seven in ten of the bank transfer scams reported last year.

When buying, try not to pay by bank transfer for something you haven't seen. If the item never turns up, it's very hard to get the money back. Where you can, see the item and pay in person, or use a payment method with buyer protection. Be wary of prices that seem too good to be true, sellers who push you to pay a deposit to "hold" something, or say lots of other people are interested. Have a look at the seller's profile: a new account with no friends or reviews is a warning sign.

When selling, watch out for buyers who say they'll send a courier to collect the item and pay you through a link. These links lead to fake payment pages that steal your card details. Only accept a payment you can see for yourself in your own bank app. And never give anyone a code that's been sent to your phone. A buyer asking for a code "to check you're real" is trying to take over your account.

If you're meeting up, choose a busy public place, take someone with you if you can, and check the item works before you hand over any money.

If you're not sure about a buyer or seller, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help you check.
