Get Online Week Day 7

By James Gartshore · 25 October 2026 · Getting online and staying connected
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, it's the last day of Get Online Week, so I wanted to finish by sharing where you can get free help with technology, because you don't have to work it all out on your own.

AbilityNet is a charity that offers free tech help for older and disabled people. Their friendly volunteers can help over the phone or even visit you at home, with anything from setting up a new phone to sorting out problems with your computer. You can find out more at abilitynet.org.uk.

Good Things Foundation, the charity behind Get Online Week, has a map of free local places that help people get online, like community centres, libraries and charities. Search "Good Things find a centre" to find your nearest one.

Many local Age UK branches also run free tech help sessions where you can bring your phone, tablet or laptop and get help with whatever you're stuck on. Visit ageuk.org.uk or contact your local branch to find out what's on near you.

And don't forget your local library. Staff can often help with using computers, printing and getting online, and some run regular digital help sessions too.
