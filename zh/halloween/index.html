Halloween

By James Gartshore · 31 October 2026 · Everyday tech
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, with Halloween tonight, I wanted to share a few ways tech can help, whether you're looking forward to trick or treaters or would rather have a quiet night in.

If you have a smart doorbell, like Ring, Google Nest or Eufy, you can see who's at the door on your phone and talk to them through the doorbell without opening the door. Many also let you set up quick replies, so you can let callers know you're not handing out sweets this year.

If the children or grandchildren are going out trick or treating, location sharing is a great way to keep an eye on where they are. On an iPhone, the Find My app lets family members share their location with each other. On Android, open Google Maps, tap your profile picture and choose Location sharing. Just make sure everyone agrees to it, and remember to switch it off afterwards if you don't need it.

It gets dark early now, so your phone's torch is handy for lighting the way on dark pavements, paths and steps. On most phones, swipe down from the top of the screen and tap the torch.

And if you'd rather not have any callers, lots of police forces offer a free "No trick or treat" poster that you can print and put in your window. Just search for your local police force and "no trick or treat" to find it. Most trick or treaters will respect it.
