Make your battery last all day

By James Gartshore · 20 October 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, with the colder days coming in, here are a few simple ways to make your phone battery last longer, on both iPhone and Android.

The quickest one is the battery saving mode. On an iPhone, go to Settings, then Battery, and turn on Low Power Mode. On Android, it's in Settings, then Battery, and it's called Battery Saver (on Samsung phones it's called Power saving). It cuts down on things running in the background, and you can turn it off again whenever you like.

To keep the battery healthy for years, you can stop it charging all the way to 100% every night, which wears it out faster. On an iPhone, look in Settings, then Battery, then Charging (on older iPhones it's Battery Health & Charging). On Android, look in Settings, then Battery, for Battery protection or Adaptive charging.

If your battery seems to drain quickly, both phones show you which apps are using the most. On an iPhone, it's Settings, then Battery, and scroll down. On Android, it's Settings, then Battery, then Battery usage.

A few things that work on both: - Keep it cool. Heat harms batteries more than anything, so don't leave it in the sun, on a car dashboard, or under a pillow while it charges. - Turn the screen brightness down a little. The screen uses more battery than anything else. - There's no need to swipe away your apps. It doesn't save battery, and can even use more when you open them again. - Top up whenever it suits you. You don't need to let it run down to 0% first.
