Video call your family

Make, answer and end a video call on WhatsApp or FaceTime, call the whole family at once, and get a good picture.

Download and print (PDF) Practise it on a pretend phone

Free to print and share. 2 A4 pages, large print, iPhone and Android side by side.

Video call your family

See the people you love, for free, on WhatsApp or FaceTime. Tick each one off as you go.

1

Make a video call

Done

Both of you need the same app. WhatsApp works on any phone; FaceTime is iPhone to iPhone.

WhatsApp

  1. Open WhatsApp
  2. Tap the person's name to open your chat
  3. Tap the video camera at the top

FaceTime (iPhone)

  1. Open FaceTime
  2. Tap New FaceTime and type their name
  3. Tap the green FaceTime button
2

Answer a video call

Done

Your phone rings as normal, with a video camera symbol.

When the phone is locked

  1. iPhone: slide the green button to the right
  2. Android: swipe the green button up

When you're using it

  1. Tap the green Accept or Answer button
3

The buttons during a call

Done

Tap the screen once if the buttons disappear.

  • Microphone: mutes you (tap again so they can hear you)
  • Camera with arrows: switches to the back camera, to show them something
  • Red button: ends the call

4

Call the whole family at once

Done

Up to 32 people can join a WhatsApp video call.

Start a group call

  1. Open the family's group chat in WhatsApp
  2. Tap the video camera at the top
  3. Tick who to call, then tap Call

Add someone to a call

  1. During the call, tap the person with a +
  2. Choose who to add
Get a good picture. Sit facing a window or a lamp, not with it behind you. Rest the phone on something at eye level, like a pile of books, so your arm doesn't get tired.
Use your Wi-Fi at home. Video calls use a lot of mobile data. On Wi-Fi they're free.
Can't hear them? Press the volume up button on the side of the phone during the call. Can't they hear you? Check the microphone button isn't crossed out.
Stay safe: only video call people you know. If someone you don't know video calls you, don't answer. If a "relative" on a new number asks for money, ring them on the number you already have.
