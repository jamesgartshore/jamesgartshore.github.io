Keep your phone safe

Five free settings that protect your phone, your money and your photos.

1

Lock your phone

Done

A PIN, your face or your fingerprint stops anyone else getting into it.

iPhone

  1. Settings > Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode)
  2. Tap Turn Passcode On and choose 6 numbers

Android

  1. Settings > Security (on Samsung: Lock screen)
  2. Tap Screen lock and choose PIN
2

Turn on automatic updates

Done

Updates are free and fix the holes scammers use. Let the phone do them overnight.

iPhone

  1. Settings > General > Software Update
  2. Tap Automatic Updates and turn everything on

Android

  1. Settings > Software update (or System > Software updates)
  2. Turn on automatic download over Wi-Fi
3

Turn on Find My

Done

If it's lost or stolen, you can see where it is, make it ring, or lock it.

iPhone

  1. Settings > your name > Find My
  2. Tap Find My iPhone and turn it on

Android

  1. Settings > Google > Find My Device (or Find Hub)
  2. Make sure it's turned on

4

Back up your photos

Done

If the phone breaks or gets lost, your photos are kept safe online and come back on a new phone.

iPhone

  1. Settings > your name > iCloud > Photos
  2. Turn on Sync this iPhone

Android

  1. Open Google Photos
  2. Tap your picture at the top, then Turn on backup

Both have free storage to start with, and may ask you to pay for more if you have lots of photos.

5

Add your medical ID

Done

Paramedics can see your conditions, medicines and who to call, without unlocking your phone.

iPhone

  1. Open the Health app
  2. Tap your picture, then Medical ID > Edit
  3. Turn on Show When Locked

Android

  1. Settings > Safety & emergency
  2. Tap Medical information and fill it in
Never tell anyone your PIN, or a code sent to your phone by text. Your bank, Apple, Google and the police will never ask for them.
