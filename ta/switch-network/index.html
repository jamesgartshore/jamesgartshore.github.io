Switch Phone Network

By James Gartshore · 21 October 2026 · Money

Hi everyone, I wanted to share a really easy way to switch your mobile phone network and keep your number, without having to ring up and be talked into staying.

Every UK mobile network has to support free text codes. If you're not sure whether you're still in a contract, text INFO to 85075. You'll get a reply telling you if you're in contract and whether there's anything to pay if you leave. It doesn't start anything, so it's safe to try.

If you want to switch and keep your number, text PAC to 65075. You'll get a text back with a code, which lasts 30 days. Give it to your new provider when you sign up and they'll move your number across, usually within one working day. Your old contract ends automatically, so you don't need to cancel it yourself. If you'd rather have a new number, text STAC to 75075 instead. Some networks ask for your date of birth or account PIN in the text, and family accounts with several numbers need to request the code online or by phone.

It's worth checking because a lot of people are still paying the full monthly price after their contract has ended, even though their phone is paid off. A SIM-only deal can cost a fraction of the price. Comparison sites show the deals available, and if you get benefits like Universal Credit or Pension Credit, ask about a social tariff, which is a cheaper deal for people on certain benefits.

If you'd like help finding a better deal or switching, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.
