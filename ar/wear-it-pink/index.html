ارتدِ اللون الوردي

By James Gartshore · 23 October 2026 · Health and wellbeing
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, today is Wear It Pink day for Breast Cancer Now, so I wanted to share some free ways your phone can help remind you to check your chest regularly.

Checking yourself regularly means you're more likely to notice any changes early, but it's also easy to forget. CoppaFeel! is a charity that runs a free monthly text reminder service. Once you sign up at coppafeel.org, you'll get a friendly text each month reminding you to check.

Know Your Lemons is a free app that shows you the signs to look out for and how to check, step by step, using simple pictures. It can also send you monthly reminders.

If you'd rather not sign up to anything, you can set a repeating reminder yourself. Open the Calendar or Reminders app on your phone, add a reminder, and set it to repeat every month on the same day.

The NHS website also has clear information about signs and symptoms and about breast screening. Just search "NHS breast screening". The NHS invites women aged 50 to 71 for breast screening every 3 years. If you're over 71, you won't get an invitation automatically, but you can still ask your GP or local screening service for one.

If you notice any changes, contact your GP. Breast Cancer Now also has a free helpline on 0808 800 6000 if you'd like to talk to a nurse.

If you'd like help setting up a reminder on your phone, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.

I hope this helps, and please pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
