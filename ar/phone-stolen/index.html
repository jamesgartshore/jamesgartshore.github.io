Phone Stolen

By James Gartshore · 21 October 2026 · Scams and staying safe
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, I wanted to share what to do if your phone is ever lost or stolen, and a couple of things worth doing now, just in case.

Thieves often go straight for banking and payment apps, so the first thing to do is ring your bank. They can block your cards, Apple Pay or Google Wallet, and your banking app. The number is on the back of your bank card, or you can dial 159 to be put through to your bank safely. If you need to, borrow someone else's phone.

Next, lock your phone from another device. For an iPhone, go to icloud.com/find on a computer, tablet or a friend's phone. For Android, go to google.com/android/find (Google's Find Hub) and sign in with the same Google account as your phone. You can lock it, put a message on the screen with a number to call, or erase it if you don't think you'll get it back.

Then ring your mobile network. They can block the phone so it can't be used on any UK network, and stop anyone running up charges on your account. Some networks only limit what you have to pay if you tell them within 24 hours. Finally, report it to the police by ringing 101 or online, and keep the crime reference number for your insurance.

There are a few things worth doing now. Dial *#06# on your phone and it will show your IMEI number, which is like a serial number for your phone. Write it down somewhere safe, as your network will ask for it. On an iPhone, turn on Stolen Device Protection in Settings under Face ID and Passcode. On Android, look for Theft protection in Settings under Google or Security. And think about hiding message previews on your lock screen, so nobody can read your security codes without unlocking your phone.
