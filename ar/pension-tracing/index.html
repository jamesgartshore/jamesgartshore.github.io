يوم تتبع المعاشات التقاعدية

By James Gartshore · 26 October 2026 · Money
See the old version of this poster Download the old version

Hi everyone, today is National Pension Tracing Day, so I wanted to share some free government tools that can help you track down any pensions you may have lost touch with.

If you've had a few different jobs over the years, there's a good chance you've paid into a workplace pension that you've since forgotten about. Millions of pensions are thought to be lost, worth billions of pounds in total, often because people moved house or changed jobs and the pension company lost track of them.

The government's free Pension Tracing Service can help you find contact details for old workplace and personal pensions. You just need the name of the employer or pension company. You'll find it at gov.uk/find-pension-contact-details, or you can call them on 0800 731 0193.

While you're at it, you can check your State Pension forecast at gov.uk/check-state-pension. It shows how much you could get, when you can start claiming, and whether there are any gaps in your National Insurance record you might be able to fill.

If you're aged 50 or over, Pension Wise from MoneyHelper offers free, impartial guidance appointments by phone or face to face, to help you understand your options for your pension. You can book at moneyhelper.org.uk.

And please be careful of pension scams. Fraudsters often contact people offering to "unlock" pensions early, offering free pension reviews, or promising amazing returns. Before you deal with any company, you can check it on the FCA's free ScamSmart website at fca.org.uk/scamsmart.

Never pay a company to find a lost pension for you. The government service does it for free.

If you'd like help tracking down an old pension, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.
