Privacy

This is a personal site, run by me, James Gartshore, in my spare time. I collect as little as I can, I don't show adverts, and I never sell or share your details.

In short: no cookies, no tracking you around the internet, and the only personal details I get are the ones you choose to send me.

Counting visits

I use GoatCounter to see how many people read each tip, so I know which ones are helpful. It records the page you viewed, the website you came from, your type of browser and screen size, and your rough country. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't keep your IP address, and can't follow you onto other sites.

The "This helped me" button on each tip adds one to a simple total for that tip, kept by a small counter I run on Cloudflare. Only the number is stored. Your browser remembers that you've pressed it so it only counts once, but nothing about you is sent or kept.

If you send me a message

The contact form sends your name, email address and message to my email inbox, through a service called Web3Forms. I only use your details to reply to you. I keep messages in my email for as long as they're useful, and I'll delete yours if you ask.

If you leave a comment

Comments are sent to my email through Web3Forms, and I read each one before it goes up. If I approve it, your first name, your comment and the date are shown under the tip for anyone to read. Comments marked "via Nextdoor" were first posted on Nextdoor. I share them either with the person's permission or without their name, and leave out anything personal. Ask me any time and I'll take your comment down.

If you sign up for tips by email

Email sign-ups are handled by follow.it, which stores your email address so it can send you new tips. I can see the list of people signed up, but I don't use it for anything else. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and once you unsubscribe you won't get any more.

Display options

If you change the text size, colours or other display options, your choice is saved in your own browser so it's remembered next time. The same goes for your progress on the free courses and the name on your certificate. None of it is ever sent to me. If you turn on the easy-read font, it's downloaded from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address, as with any website that uses its fonts.

Listen and share buttons

"Listen to this tip" uses your phone or computer's own reading voice. Some browsers, such as Chrome, offer online voices, which send the text to that company to be read out. The share buttons don't send anything until you tap one, and then they open WhatsApp, Facebook or your email as normal.

Where the site is hosted

The site is hosted for free on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may briefly log visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure. You can read more in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I have about you, and ask me to correct or delete it, using the contact form. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
