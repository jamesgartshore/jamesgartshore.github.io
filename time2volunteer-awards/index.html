Shortlisted for the Time2Volunteer Awards 2026

By James Gartshore · 08 October 2026 · News

Hi everyone, I wanted to share a bit of news. I was recently shortlisted in the Time2Volunteer Awards 2026 for the volunteering I do with a local tech support group, helping people with their phones, computers and getting online.

I didn't win in the end, but it was a real honour just to be nominated, and it was lovely to read what had been written about why I was put forward.

Helping people feel more confident with technology is something I really enjoy, and it's a big part of why I share these tech tips on here. Whether it's spotting a scam text, setting up the NHS App or just making the writing on your phone bigger, I know how much of a difference the little things can make.

So thank you to everyone who reads, comments on and shares my posts. It really does mean a lot, and I'll keep them coming.

As always, if there's ever anything you'd like help with, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.
