Dyspraxia: tools to stay organised

By James Gartshore · 13 October 2026 · Health and wellbeing

In short

  • Shared family calendar: Everyone sees what's on and when, from clubs to appointments. (Free: Google Calendar or iPhone Calendar)
  • Microsoft To Do: Simple tick-off lists for packing a bag or getting ready in the morning. (Free app)
  • Google Keep: Notes and checklists with photos, like a picture of a packed PE bag. (Free app)
  • Smart speaker routines: Say "good morning" and it reads out the plan for the day. (Amazon Alexa or Google Home app)

Hi everyone, it's day two of Dyspraxia Awareness Week, and today I wanted to share some free tools that help with staying organised.

Dyspraxia doesn't just affect movement and coordination. It can also make planning, remembering things and keeping track of time much harder, for children and adults alike. The good news is that your phone can take a lot of that pressure off.

A shared family calendar is a great place to start. With Google Calendar or the Calendar app on an iPhone, you can create a calendar the whole family can see, so everyone knows what's happening and when, from after-school clubs to dentist appointments. You can add reminders to each event too, so nobody has to rely on remembering.

For everyday routines, a simple checklist app like Microsoft To Do can really help. You can make lists for things like packing a school bag or getting ready in the morning, and tick each item off as you go. It's free, and it's satisfying to see everything ticked.

Google Keep is another free app that's brilliant for checklists, because you can add photos to your notes. A picture of a fully packed PE bag, for example, makes it really easy to check nothing's been forgotten, especially for younger children who find written lists harder.

If you have an Amazon Echo or Google speaker, you can also set up routines. You choose a phrase like "good morning", and the speaker can read out the weather, what's on the calendar and any reminders for the day, all in one go.

If you'd like help setting any of these up, just send me a message and I'll do my best to help.

I hope this helps, and feel free to pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
