Add a Bank Card to Google Wallet

By James Gartshore · 26 October 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, did you know you can pay with your phone instead of your bank card? Adding your card to Google Wallet means you can still pay if you've left your purse or wallet at home, and it's actually safer than using the card itself.

1. Open the Google Wallet app. If you don't have it, it's free from the Play Store. 2. Tap "Add to Wallet", then Payment card. 3. Hold your bank card up to the camera, or type in the details. 4. Your bank checks it's really you, usually with a code sent by text or a tap in your banking app. 5. To pay, unlock your phone and hold it near the card reader, just like tapping your card.

Why it's worth doing: - The shop never sees your real card number. - Nobody can pay with it unless they can unlock your phone. - You can still pay if you forget your purse or wallet. - You can use it on buses that take contactless.

On an iPhone, the Wallet app works the same way.
