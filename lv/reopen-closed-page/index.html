Reopen a Closed Web Page

By James Gartshore · 21 October 2026 · Everyday tech

Hi everyone, have you ever closed an important web page on your phone by mistake, like a form you were filling in or a page you needed? Don't panic, you can usually get it back in a few taps.

On an Android phone using Chrome (the usual web browser): 1. Open Chrome again. 2. Tap the 3 dots at the top right of the screen. 3. Tap History. 4. You'll see a list of the pages you've looked at, newest at the top. Tap the one you want and it opens again.

If you use Samsung Internet instead, tap the 3 lines at the bottom of the screen, then History.

On an iPhone using Safari, tap the book icon at the bottom, then the clock icon to see your history.
